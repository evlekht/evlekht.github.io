Privacy Policy — Ubuntu Backup

Ubuntu Backup is a personal-use backup setup operated solely by its owner. It uses Restic and Rclone to store encrypted backups in the owner's own Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission, which limits access to files the application creates or is explicitly granted access to. This access is used only to create, read, update, and delete files for the owner's encrypted backups.

Data handling

Restic encrypts backup contents on the owner's computer before upload. Encrypted backups are stored in the owner's Google Drive. Rclone OAuth tokens and backup encryption credentials remain on the owner's devices; they are not placed on this website.

This setup does not collect analytics, use tracking cookies, sell data, or send Google user data to any additional application server. Google processes files stored in Google Drive according to its own terms and policies.

Retention and deletion

The owner controls backup retention and may delete the backed-up files from Google Drive and revoke OAuth access through Google Account settings.
